# Rapido-Ride-Analytics-Dashboard-Power-BI-
Rapido Ride Analytics Dashboard – Power BI Report

This Power BI report offers a comprehensive analysis of Rapido's ride-hailing operations, covering booking trends, rider behavior, vehicle performance, revenue insights, and location-based patterns. Built on a structured data model with dedicated fact and dimension tables, the report transforms raw booking data into actionable business intelligence across six interactive pages: Home, Overview, Revenue, Rider, Vehicle, and Location.

Key areas covered:

Booking Performance: Tracks total bookings, completed rides, cancelled rides (by both customer and driver), and incomplete rides, along with detailed cancellation reasons to help identify operational bottlenecks.
Revenue Analysis: Monitors total booking value, average ride distance, and payment method distribution to surface revenue drivers and trends over time.
Rider Insights: Segments customers into first-time, returning, and regular riders, enabling analysis of customer retention and loyalty patterns alongside customer satisfaction through ride ratings.
Vehicle Analytics: Breaks down performance by vehicle type (e.g., bike, auto, sedan, intercity comfort), supported by custom vehicle icons for enhanced visual clarity.
Location Intelligence: Analyzes pickup and drop locations to reveal geographic demand patterns and popular routes.
Time-Based Trends: Leverages dedicated date and time-slot tables to enable drill-down analysis by year, quarter, month, and time-of-day, helping identify peak demand periods.

Technical highlights:

Custom DAX measures for booking counts, completed/lost bookings, booking value, average distance, cancellation contribution percentage, and rider segmentation (first-time, return, regular).
Driver and customer rating fields integrated to assess service quality.
Dedicated calendar and date tables supporting robust time intelligence calculations.
Custom visual theming and icon-based UI elements for an intuitive, branded user experience.
